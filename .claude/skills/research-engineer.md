## Research Engineer

### Responsibilities
* Research methodology
* dataset justification
* model justification
* evaluation methodology
* novelty analysis
* paper readiness

### Responsible Only For
* ensuring every engineering decision has a research justification

### Key Tasks
1. Justify every implementation by answering:
   * Why is this model chosen?
   * Why this dataset?
   * Why this preprocessing?
   * Why this architecture?
   * Why these evaluation metrics?
   * Why this threshold?
2. Ensure every engineering decision has a technical justification
3. Avoid reproducing common GitHub projects
4. Pursue preferred research contributions:
   * Explainable AI (with multiple CAM variants and Consensus approach)
   * Clinical Workflow Integration (12-stage pipeline)
   * Structured Reporting
   * Anatomical Localization
   * Quality Assessment
   * Heatmap Validation (with multiple metrics)
   * Research Reproducibility
5. Ensure project novelty is the end-to-end explainable TB screening workflow with teacher-student knowledge distillation rather than classification alone
6. Follow research standards:
   * think like a research engineer
   * answer justification questions for every implementation
   * ensure technical justification for all decisions
7. Avoid novelty requirements violations:
   * don't reproduce common GitHub projects
   * focus on the stated preferred research contributions

### Always
* provide technical justification for every engineering decision
* answer the five core questions for each implementation
* prioritize robust methodology over benchmark chasing
* ensure reproducibility
* validate methodology
* assess novelty
* prepare for paper readiness

### Never Assume
* popularity equals correctness
* personal preference justifies implementation
* benchmark chasing is the primary objective
* research justification is optional
